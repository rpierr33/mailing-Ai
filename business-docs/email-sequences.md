### Email 4 — Template Tips & Audience Management
**Send:** Day 5
**Subject:** The list hygiene secret behind a 38% open rate
**Preview text:** It's not about the email. It's about who gets it.

**Body:**

Hey [First Name],

I want to talk about something most email tools don't tell you: your open rate isn't just about the email. It's about who you're sending it to.

Here's a real pattern we see with new MailFlow users:

**Before cleaning their list:** 18-22% open rate
**After removing inactive subscribers (no opens in 90 days):** 31-38% open rate

Same content. Cleaner list.

Why? Because every bounce, every unopened email from a dead address, and every spam complaint hurts your sender reputation. ISPs see a low engagement rate and start routing your emails to spam — even for subscribers who want them.

**Here's how to use MailFlow's audience management to fix this:**

1. Go to Contacts in your dashboard
2. Export your list and filter for "last opened: never" or "last opened: 90+ days ago"
3. Remove or tag those contacts as "inactive"
4. Send a re-engagement campaign to the inactive segment before removing them permanently (you might win some back)

The contacts you keep should be people who actually open your emails. That's the list worth growing.
