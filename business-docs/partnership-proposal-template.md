## General Partnership Principles

1. **Lead with value for their audience, not for us.** Every proposal starts with what their users get, not our metrics.
2. **Be specific about the ask.** Vague "partnership" emails get ignored. Name the deliverable, the timeline, and the budget expectation.
3. **Give before you ask.** Free Pro accounts. Free content. Free demo. Make the first move.
4. **Short emails win.** Every template above is under 300 words. Keep it there.
5. **One ask per email.** Don't stack three options in the first message. Pick the one you want most.
