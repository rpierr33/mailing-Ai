# MailFlow AI — 10-Minute Product Demo Script

**Audience:** Potential users, creators, investors, partners
**Format:** Screen share or pre-recorded video
**Goal:** Show — don't tell. Every claim gets demonstrated live.

---

## Setup Before Starting

- Log in as demo account (demo@mailflow.ai / demo credentials)
- Clear any modal dialogs
- Have these ready: a one-sentence AI prompt, a sample campaign name, a test email address for preview
- Window: 1440px wide, Chrome, dark mode OS
- Record at 1080p minimum if making a video

## Minute 1:00 — 3:30: Create a Campaign with AI

**[The campaign wizard opens]**

"I'm going to create a complete email campaign right now. Watch the clock.

First: name it. I'll call this 'Summer Sale Launch.'"

**[Type the campaign name]**

"Now: subject line. I could type one manually. But I'm going to use the AI."

**[Click into the subject line field — the AI generator panel opens or activate the AI button]**

"I'll type one sentence describing what this email is about."

**[Type: "Write a subject line for a summer flash sale — 30% off everything for 48 hours, casual and exciting"]**

**[Click Generate — wait for the result]**

"There it is. In under 10 seconds.

[Read the generated subject line aloud.]

That's good copy. It has urgency, it has specificity, it doesn't feel like spam. I didn't write it — I described what I wanted.

Now watch what happens when I switch the tone."

**[Switch tone dropdown from Casual to Storytelling, click Regenerate]**

"Different angle. Same prompt. The AI isn't just filling a template — it's rewriting with a different intent.

Now the email body."

**[Navigate to the email body / content section]**

"Same process. One sentence."

**[Type: "Write a promotional email body for a summer flash sale, 30% off all products for 48 hours, casual tone, strong CTA to shop now"]**

**[Click Generate]**

"[Let it generate — read the output aloud briefly]

Full email. Subject. Preview text. Body with a clear CTA. Done in about 90 seconds total.

This is the workflow. Describe it, generate it, edit if you want, send."

## Demo Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Keep cursor movement deliberate — slow down on key interactions
- Read generated AI copy aloud — let the audience absorb it
- Narrate what you're clicking BEFORE you click it
- Keep energy up, especially in the middle section

**Don't:**
- Rush through the AI generation — that's the hero moment
- Skip the mobile preview — it's a signal of quality
- Apologize for anything or say "usually this works better" — fix the demo environment beforehand
- Show empty states in analytics without context — use seeded demo data
