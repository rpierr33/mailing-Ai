## Hunter Strategy

**Option A: Self-hunt (recommended for launch)**
- You control the timing
- No dependency on someone else's schedule
- Downside: Top Hunters have established audiences who discover and engage early

**Option B: Top Hunter**
Reach out to active Product Hunt hunters with 500+ hunts and 50K+ followers. They often hunt products for free if they find it genuinely interesting.

Finding hunters: go to producthunt.com/hunters and sort by follower count. Look for hunters active in: AI, Marketing Tools, Productivity, SaaS.

DM template for Top Hunter:

"Hey [hunter name] — I'm launching MailFlow AI, an AI-native email marketing platform. Type one sentence, get a complete email in 10 seconds. $29/month, unlimited contacts, never charges for unsubscribes. Genuinely differentiated from Mailchimp/ConvertKit. Would you be interested in hunting it? Happy to share a demo or Pro access."

## First Comment (Post at 12:01am Exactly)

The first comment is the most important content you'll write for this launch. It appears at the top of your product page. It's your pitch, your story, and your differentiator — all in one. Write it now. Don't improvise at midnight.

I'm here all day — ask me anything about the product, the tech stack (Next.js 15, Anthropic Claude, Resend), the pricing philosophy, or whatever you want to dig into.

Let's go.

## Realistic Expectations

| Outcome | Upvotes | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 100-200 | Listed, some signups, brand credibility |
| Target | 400-600 | Top 5, strong signup spike, newsletter coverage |
| Best case | 800+ | Product of the Day, press pickup, viral moment |

Even a "conservative" launch on Product Hunt with 100-200 upvotes generates: a permanent listing, a credibility badge, and a few dozen qualified signups. The baseline is positive. The upside is significant.

Product Hunt is a visibility amplifier and credibility signal — not primarily a customer acquisition channel. The signups matter; the badge matters more for long-term trust.
