# User Onboarding Walkthrough — MailFlow AI (2-Minute Screen Recording)

**Format:** Screen recording, 1920x1080, 2:00–2:15 runtime
**Purpose:** In-app help video, welcome email embed, YouTube tutorial
**Audience:** Brand new users — just signed up, first session
**Tone:** Clear and encouraging — not talking down. Assume the viewer is smart.
**Activation goal (the "aha moment"):** User sends or previews their first AI-generated email

---

## The Activation Framework

Per onboarding-flow principles, max 5 steps, each must feel valuable not bureaucratic:

1. Sign up or demo login
2. Add your first contact
3. Create your first campaign with AI
4. Preview the email
5. Send (or schedule)

The viewer should feel: "I could do this in 5 minutes." They should be right.

---

## SCENE 1 — SIGN UP / DEMO LOGIN (0:00–0:25)

**What to show:** The MailFlow AI landing page or login page.

**Two paths — show Path B for recording purposes:**

Path A (new user): Landing page → Sign Up → Enter email + password → Redirect to dashboard
Path B (demo): Landing page → "Try the demo" or "Continue with demo account" → Instant dashboard access

**Recording uses Path B (demo login) — it's faster and shows value immediately.**

**Actions:**
1. Start on the MailFlow landing page
2. Click "Try for free" or "Start free"
3. Enter credentials OR click demo access
4. Dashboard loads

**Narration:**
"Sign up takes 30 seconds. Or try the demo — no account needed."

**On-screen text overlay:**
"No credit card. No setup."

**Why this matters:** The first 30 seconds of a new tool experience determines whether someone explores or closes the tab. Get to the dashboard fast.

---

## SCENE 2 — ADD YOUR FIRST CONTACT (0:25–0:55)

**What to show:** Contacts page. Adding one contact manually. Tagging them.

**Actions:**
1. Click "Contacts" in the left navigation
2. Click "Add Contact" button
3. Fill in the form — move deliberately so viewer can follow:
   - First name: "Alex"
   - Last name: "Rivera"
   - Email: "alex@example.com"
   - Tag: "Subscribers"
4. Click Save / Add
5. Contact appears in the list with a subtle animation

**Narration:**
"Add your first contact. Name, email, tag. Done."

**On-screen text overlay:**
- On the empty contacts state: "You'll grow this. Start with one."
- After save: "Your list. You own it."

**What NOT to show:**
- CSV import (save for a follow-up video)
- Advanced contact fields
- Segmentation rules

Keep it to one contact, one tag, one click. The "aha" here is how simple the data model is.

---

## SCENE 3 — CREATE FIRST CAMPAIGN WITH AI (0:55–1:35)

**The centerpiece of the walkthrough. This is where the value lands.**

**Actions:**
1. Click "Campaigns" in the navigation
2. Click "New Campaign"
3. Type a campaign title: "My First Campaign"
4. Navigate to the AI content section

**The AI generation (1:05–1:30):**
5. Find the AI prompt field
6. Type clearly and slowly: "Welcome my new subscriber and tell them what my newsletter is about — design tips for indie makers"
7. Hover over the tone selector — briefly show the 4 options
8. Select "Casual" tone
9. Click Generate
10. Watch in real time: subject line appears → preview text → body
11. Pause on the result for 5 full seconds

**Narration:**
"One sentence. Hit generate."
[Pause during generation]
"There it is. Subject, preview text, full email."
[Pause on result]
"That's your welcome email. AI wrote it. You'll edit it."

**On-screen text overlays:**
- On the prompt field: "Describe your email in one sentence."
- During generation: "Claude AI is writing..."
- On the result: "Edit what doesn't sound like you."

**Why this is the aha moment:**
New users often spend hours writing their first email and never send it. Showing that a complete, usable email exists in under 10 seconds removes the biggest friction point in email marketing onboarding.

---

## SCENE 4 — PREVIEW THE EMAIL (1:35–1:50)

**What to show:** The preview — how the email looks in an inbox simulator or preview pane.

**Actions:**
1. Click "Preview" button (or equivalent)
2. Show the email rendering — dark template, formatted text, visible CTA button
3. Briefly scroll through the preview
4. Point out (cursor hover): the subject line and preview text at the top — "this is what they see before they open"

**Narration:**
"Preview it before you send. This is exactly what they'll see."

**On-screen text overlay:**
"See it as your reader sees it."

---

## SCENE 5 — SEND OR SCHEDULE (1:50–2:05)

**What to show:** The send step. Keep it simple.

**Actions:**
1. Navigate back from preview
2. Click "Send" or "Review & Send"
3. Show the confirmation screen:
   - To: 1 contact (Alex Rivera)
   - Subject: [generated subject line]
   - Send now vs Schedule toggle
4. For the walkthrough: click "Send Now"
5. Success state: confirmation animation or toast — "Campaign sent!"

**Narration:**
"One contact. Hit send. That's it."
[On confirmation]
"Your first campaign is out."

**On-screen text overlay:**
"That's email marketing."

---

## SCENE 6 — CHECK ANALYTICS (2:05–2:15)

**Brief closer — plants the seed for return visits.**

**Actions:**
1. Navigate to Analytics
2. Show the campaign stats (even if 0s — show where the data will live)
3. Brief hover over open rate and click rate sections

**Narration:**
"Analytics update as your subscribers open and click. Check back tomorrow."

**On-screen text overlay:**
"Real-time. No refresh needed."

**Final frame:**
Dashboard with a soft glow effect.
Text: "You're set up. Go write something worth reading."

## Production Notes

- Use demo account with real seeded data so the analytics section looks populated
- Do NOT skip errors silently — if something doesn't work in the recording, stop and re-record cleanly
- Total runtime: aim for 2:00–2:10. Under 2 minutes is better than over.
- Cursor: large, slow, deliberate movements. No jitter.
- Record twice: once with VO narration, once as caption-only (silent) — use the silent version for the in-app embedded video
- Export formats:
  - 1920x1080 (landing page / YouTube)
  - 1080x1080 square crop of Scenes 2-4 (LinkedIn / Instagram)
- After recording: add a chapter card at the end: "Next: Import your existing contact list →"
