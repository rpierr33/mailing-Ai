## The Problem (30 seconds)

Email marketing has the highest ROI of any digital channel — $36 returned for every $1 spent. Every creator, every small business owner, every indie hacker knows they should be using it.

So why aren't more of them? Because the tools built to do it are working against them.

Mailchimp charges you for unsubscribed contacts — people who asked to leave your list. ConvertKit costs $49/month past 1,000 subscribers. Every platform makes you write every email from scratch, which takes 2-3 hours most business owners don't have.

The result: email marketing stays on the "someday" list for the people who need it most. Not because the channel doesn't work, but because the tooling is slow, expensive, and designed for marketing teams — not solo operators.

---

## The Solution (30 seconds)

MailFlow AI is email marketing rebuilt from the ground up for how creators and small business owners actually work.

You type one sentence — your campaign goal, your offer, your message. The AI generates a complete email campaign: subject line, preview text, full body copy, in your chosen tone. Professional, casual, persuasive, or playful.

Review it, edit if you want, and send. The entire process takes under five minutes.

No templates to fight with. No blank page. No three hours of writing. Just describe what you want to say and let the AI say it well.

## What We've Built

MailFlow AI is a live, deployed product with a working free tier.

**Core product:**
- AI email writer — full campaigns (subject line, preview text, body) from a one-sentence prompt, powered by Claude AI. Four tone modes: Professional, Casual, Persuasive, Playful.
- Audience management — contact import, tagging, segmentation by behavior and custom fields.
- Campaign builder — dark-mode glassmorphism UI, mobile-first, campaign scheduling.
- Real-time analytics — open rates, click rates, audience growth charts, click heatmaps.
- A/B testing and automation — available on the Pro tier.

**Tech stack:** Next.js 15, TypeScript, Anthropic Claude AI, Resend (email delivery), NextAuth 5.

**Status:** Live product. Free tier active. Pro tier available at $29/month.

## How It Works

**For the user:**
1. Sign up free — no credit card, no trial clock
2. Import contacts (CSV) or add manually
3. Click "New Campaign," type one sentence describing your goal
4. Select tone (Professional / Casual / Persuasive / Playful)
5. AI generates full email in under 10 seconds
6. Edit, schedule, or send immediately
7. Track opens, clicks, and engagement in the analytics dashboard

**The AI loop:** Users who see strong campaign results create more campaigns. More campaigns mean more data and more platform engagement, which drives upgrade conversion from Free to Pro.
