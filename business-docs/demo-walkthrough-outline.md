# Demo Walkthrough Outline — MailFlow AI (3-Minute Screen Recording)

**Format:** Screen recording, 1920x1080, 3:00–3:15 runtime
**Purpose:** Product Hunt gallery video, landing page hero demo, YouTube description resource
**Audience:** First-time visitors — evaluating whether to sign up
**Energy:** Confident and unhurried. This is a tool that doesn't need to rush.
**Tool:** Loom, QuickTime, or OBS — clean, no cursor jitter
**Cursor:** Use a large visible cursor. Slow deliberate movements.

---

## Pre-Recording Setup

- Log in as the demo account (`demo@mailflowai.com`) — this account has seeded data: 3 existing campaigns, 247 contacts, analytics populated
- Set browser to Chrome, 100% zoom, no bookmarks bar
- Dark OS theme — window chrome matches the product
- Close all other tabs — only MailFlow open
- Full screen mode
- Music: Record silence — add lo-fi track in post at 20% volume

---

## SCENE 1 — DASHBOARD (0:00–0:30)

**What to show:** The MailFlow dashboard loads. Pause 3 seconds to let it breathe.

**Narration (or on-screen caption):**
"This is MailFlow AI. Dark mode. Built for creators."

**Actions:**
- Hover over the three campaign cards briefly — show open rate and click rate stats
- Slowly scroll down to see the analytics section (summary stats visible)
- No clicking yet — just orient the viewer

**Why this scene matters:** First impressions. The dark glassmorphism UI is the visual differentiator. Let it land.

**On-screen text overlay:**
"Built on Next.js. Powered by Claude AI."

---

## SCENE 2 — CREATE CAMPAIGN (0:30–1:30)

**This is the money scene. Take your time.**

**Actions:**
1. Click "New Campaign" button (top right)
2. A modal or new page loads — show the campaign title field
3. Type a campaign title: "Spring Template Launch"
4. Navigate to the AI content generation section

**The AI Generation (0:50–1:20) — the most important 30 seconds in the demo:**

5. Find the AI prompt input field
6. Type slowly, deliberately: "Announce my new Notion template pack to my audience — playful but professional tone"
7. Pause before hitting generate — let the viewer read the prompt
8. Click Generate
9. Watch the AI write in real time — DO NOT speed this up
10. Zoom in gently (browser zoom to 125%) as the content appears
11. Show: Subject line generates first → Preview text → Email body
12. Pause on the finished result for 5 seconds

**Narration:**
"One sentence. That's all it takes."
[Pause on result]
"Subject line. Preview text. Full email body."

**Then show tone toggle:**
13. Click "Storytelling" tone mode
14. Click Regenerate
15. Watch the email change character — different voice, same topic
16. Brief pause: "Four tones. Match your voice."

**On-screen text overlays:**
- When typing the prompt: "Describe what you want to say."
- When it generates: "Done."
- On tone toggle: "Your voice. Not a generic AI voice."

## SCENE 3 — TEMPLATE BUILDER (1:30–2:00)

**Actions:**
1. Navigate to the template/editor section of the campaign
2. Show a pre-built dark email template already populated with the AI content
3. Click a text block — show it's editable inline
4. Drag one content block to a new position — slow, satisfying drag-and-drop
5. Click a button element — show the CTA text is editable
6. Don't over-demonstrate — 30 seconds max

**Narration:**
"Template builder. Drag and drop. Nothing breaks."

**On-screen text overlay:**
"Drag. Drop. Done."

5. Navigate back to contacts list

## SCENE 5 — ANALYTICS + HEATMAP (2:25–2:50)

**Actions:**

4. Navigate to campaign-level analytics — click on the "Spring Launch" seeded campaign
5. Show the click heatmap — the email layout with heat zones highlighted
6. Slowly move cursor over the heatmap to show it's interactive
7. Pause on the heatmap for 5 seconds — this is visually impressive

**Narration:**
"Analytics that actually tell you something. See exactly what they clicked."
