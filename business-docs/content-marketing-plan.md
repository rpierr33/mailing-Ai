# MailFlow AI — Content Marketing Plan

**Objective:** Drive organic traffic through SEO-targeted blog content, establish topical authority in the email marketing space, and create a content engine that compounds over time.

**Target quarter:** Q2-Q4 2026 (12 posts, first 3 months)

**Primary keyword clusters:**
- "best email marketing tool 2026" — commercial intent, high competition, high value
- "mailchimp alternative free" — commercial intent, high intent to switch
- "AI email writer" — emerging keyword, growing fast
- "email marketing for creators" — niche, low competition, high conversion
- "ConvertKit alternative" — high intent, underserved by quality content

**Content principles:**
- Lead with real answers. No 2,000-word introductions.
- Include actual screenshots and product context where relevant
- SEO-first structure (H1, H2 keywords, FAQ schema where applicable)
- Every post links internally to at least 2 other posts and to the signup page
- Target 1,500-2,500 words for pillar posts, 800-1,200 for supporting posts
- Evergreen priority: 70% evergreen, 30% timely (2026-specific angles)

## SEO and Distribution Plan

### On-Page SEO Requirements (every post)

- Target keyword in H1 (title)
- Target keyword in first 100 words
- Target keyword in at least 2 H2 headings
- Meta description: 150-160 characters, includes target keyword and a benefit
- Schema markup applied (type matches content)
- Internal links: minimum 2 per post, including one to signup page
- Alt text on all images
- Table of contents for posts over 1,500 words
- FAQ section at the end (4-6 questions, targets "people also ask" features)

### Publishing Schedule (Months 1-3)

| Month | Posts | Primary Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | Posts 1, 2, 3, 7 | best email marketing tools, mailchimp alternative, AI email writer, grow email list |
| Month 2 | Posts 4, 5, 6, 9 | email automation, ConvertKit alternative, small business email, subject lines |
| Month 3 | Posts 8, 10, 11, 12 | email for Etsy, open rates, free tools, indie hackers |

### Content Distribution per Post

1. Publish to blog (primary SEO value)
2. LinkedIn post — share the key insight or surprising data point from the post
3. Twitter/X thread — pull 5 key points, thread format
4. Reddit — post a value summary in the most relevant subreddit (no direct link in title)
5. Email newsletter — include in weekly digest for subscribers
6. Internal links — update 2 existing posts to link to the new post

### Backlink Acquisition

- **Target:** 3 quality backlinks per post within 90 days of publish
- **Method:** Email outreach to 10 relevant sites per post (newsletters, marketing blogs, creator publications)
- **Pitch:** "I noticed you cover email marketing tools. We just published [title] — it might be useful context for your readers."
- **Best prospects for MailFlow content:** Morning Brew creator economy coverage, Indie Hackers post, Creator Economy newsletter, MarTech Alliance, The Information (email marketing beats)
