## MailFlow AI Launches the First AI-Native Email Marketing Platform Built for Creators and the Next Generation of Small Business Owners

*Kalocode's new platform generates complete email campaigns from a single sentence — and never charges for unsubscribed contacts*

**[City], April 2026** — Kalocode today announced the public launch of MailFlow AI, an AI-powered email marketing platform designed for creators, solo entrepreneurs, and small business owners who find existing tools too slow, too expensive, or built for the wrong audience.

MailFlow AI is the first email marketing platform where AI is the primary creation workflow rather than a bolt-on feature. Users type one sentence describing their campaign goal — an offer, an announcement, a newsletter theme — and MailFlow generates a complete email campaign in under 10 seconds. This includes the subject line, preview text, and full body copy, in one of four distinct tones: Professional, Casual, Persuasive, or Playful.

The platform launches today with a free tier requiring no credit card — 500 contacts, 1,000 emails per month, and five AI campaign generations per day. Pro is available at $29 per month for unlimited contacts, unlimited AI generations, A/B testing, automation, and full analytics.

### A Different Set of Rules

MailFlow AI was built to reverse several industry practices that frustrate small-scale email marketers.

Most email marketing platforms charge for unsubscribed contacts — people who have actively opted out of a list but are still counted toward a billing tier. At 1,200 subscribers, this pricing model costs Mailchimp users $45 per month. MailFlow commits to never charging for unsubscribed contacts at any plan level.

The platform's interface is dark mode by default — an intentional decision based on the reality that most creators and young business owners do their marketing work in the evening, on devices set to dark mode. The mobile-first campaign builder is functional on phone screens, not just desktop.

"Every email marketing tool I tried was designed for a marketing team of twelve, not someone running their business from a laptop," said Ralph Pierre, founder of Kalocode and MailFlow AI. "The pricing punished you for growing. The interface required training. And writing every email from scratch was a three-hour job most people just didn't do. MailFlow turns that into a ten-minute job. We built it because we needed it, and we think a lot of other people do too."

### How It Works

The core workflow has four steps:

1. Create a new campaign in the MailFlow dashboard
2. Type one sentence — the campaign goal or message
3. Select a tone (Professional, Casual, Persuasive, or Playful)
4. Review and send the AI-generated email, or schedule it

The AI generation is powered by Anthropic's Claude model and produces output tailored for email marketing — benefit-led body copy, subject lines optimized for open rates, and preview text that complements rather than duplicates the subject line.

Analytics are included on the free tier, covering open rates, click rates, and audience growth. Pro unlocks click heatmaps showing exactly where in each email subscribers engage.

### Competitive Context

MailFlow AI enters a $12.6 billion global email marketing software market projected to reach $23.9 billion by 2030. The market is currently dominated by tools that were architected before AI became viable as a writing assistant — Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Brevo, and Beehiiv each offer varying degrees of AI assistance but none treat AI as the primary creation mode.

Where Mailchimp charges $45 per month for 1,000 contacts and ConvertKit charges $49 per month, MailFlow Pro is $29 per month for unlimited contacts. Where competitors charge for unsubscribed contacts, MailFlow does not. Where competitors default to light mode interfaces, MailFlow defaults to dark.

"The gap in this market isn't features — every tool has features. The gap is who the product is actually built for," Pierre said. "Creators and solo business owners are not the target customer for legacy email platforms. They're an afterthought. MailFlow was built specifically for them, with their workflow in mind."
