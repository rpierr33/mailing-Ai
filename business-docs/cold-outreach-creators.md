# Outreach Campaign: Newsletter Creators, Bloggers, and Independent Writers

## Target
Newsletter writers, bloggers, course creators, and coaches with 500–50,000 subscribers. Currently paying Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or Beehiiv. Active on X/Twitter, Indie Hackers, or YouTube. Self-identified as frustrated with rising email platform costs.

**Segment size:** ~2M creators globally fit this profile. Initial target: 200 direct outreach contacts for launch campaign.

## Channel
Email (primary). LinkedIn DM (secondary for those with active LinkedIn presence). Multi-touch, 5-message sequence.

## Personalization hooks
Before sending any email: check their newsletter, recent posts on X, LinkedIn, or their personal site. Note their subscriber count (if public), what they write about, and any public complaint about email tool pricing. Tier 1 personalization (named signal) preferred — do not send a Tier 3 blast.

**Research checklist per contact:**
- [ ] What is their newsletter/blog about?
- [ ] How large is their audience (public if mentioned on site, bio, or social)?
- [ ] What email platform are they currently using (check footer of their newsletter)?
- [ ] Any recent public post complaining about Mailchimp/ConvertKit pricing?
- [ ] Any recent content about email marketing, audience building, or creator business?

## Sequence

### Email 1 — Day 1: The pricing question

**Subject:** Quick question about your Mailchimp bill

**Body:**

Hey [First name],

I read [specific piece they wrote or newsletter issue]. [One sentence on what resonated — be specific, not generic.]

Quick question — what are you paying Mailchimp (or ConvertKit, or whoever) right now for your [X] subscribers?

I ask because I just launched MailFlow AI, and I'm trying to understand what the typical creator is actually spending on email tools. My bet is it's more than it should be.

If you're on Mailchimp's Essentials plan with [their approximate subscriber count], you're probably paying around $[specific estimate based on their size]. That number goes up every time your list grows — even if most of those contacts haven't opened an email in months.

Worth a quick look? [Link to MailFlow AI landing page]

No pitch. Genuinely curious what your current setup looks like.

— Ralph

P.S. If you're already on a free plan and loving it, that's totally worth knowing too.

---

**Personalization instructions:**
- Replace [specific piece they wrote] with an actual article, newsletter issue, or post title
- Estimate their Mailchimp bill based on their public subscriber count:
  - 500-1K subs: ~$13/mo (Mailchimp Essentials)
  - 1K-2.5K subs: ~$27/mo
  - 2.5K-5K subs: ~$45/mo
  - 5K-10K subs: ~$78/mo
  - 10K-50K subs: ~$135-$350/mo
- Never guess a number you can't back up

## ROI Pitch (for use in any touchpoint)

**Their current cost (ConvertKit Creator, 5K subscribers):** $99/month = $1,188/year

**MailFlow AI Pro:** $29/month = $348/year

**Savings:** $840/year — enough to pay for 2 months of Facebook ads, a freelance editor for a quarter, or a new domain and hosting stack.

**Plus:** They stop paying for unsubscribed contacts. If their list is 5K but 20% unsubscribed, they're paying ConvertKit for 1,000 people who opted out. At Pro pricing, that's ~$23/month in wasted spend.
