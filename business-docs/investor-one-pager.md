# MailFlow AI — Investor One-Pager

## The Problem

Email marketing tools are overpriced, ugly, and built for a different decade.

Mailchimp charges $45/month for 2,500 contacts — contacts you already paid to acquire. ConvertKit charges $59. Klaviyo charges $45. All three were designed before AI existed and bolted AI on as an afterthought. The result: expensive, frustrating tools that treat subscribers like a billing unit.

Meanwhile, 600 million+ creator economy participants — newsletter writers, course creators, indie hackers, small e-commerce stores — are getting squeezed. They need professional email marketing. They can't afford the incumbents. They refuse to use tools that look like they were built in 2012.

This gap is real, growing, and underserved.

---

## The Solution

**MailFlow AI** is an AI-native email marketing platform built for the next generation of marketers and creators.

One line typed into the AI. Subject line, preview text, and full email body — ready in 10 seconds. Not bolted-on AI. Not a ChatGPT button. AI as the core product.

Built for users who grew up on dark mode, glassmorphism, and instant everything. Designed to feel like a tool from 2026, not a legacy SaaS from 2014.

**What sets us apart:**
- AI writes your email from a single-sentence prompt (4 tones: professional, casual, promotional, storytelling)
- Never charges for unsubscribed contacts — competitors do
- Dark-mode-first interface that users actually want to open
- Transparent, honest pricing with no hidden fees
- Production-grade infrastructure: Next.js 15, Claude AI, Resend, auth-hardened API surface
