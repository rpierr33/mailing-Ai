# MailFlow AI — 60-Second Animated Explainer Script

**Format:** 2D motion graphics with glassmorphism UI mockups
**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (YouTube/landing page) — export 9:16 crop for TikTok/Reels
**Music:** Lo-fi electronic with an upbeat tempo lift at the 20-second mark. Think: Odesza-lite, no lyrics. Fade to quiet at CTA.
**Voice:** Young, confident. Not enthusiastic-salesperson. Like a friend who just found something good.

## Production Notes

- Keep UI mockups consistent with the actual app: dark background (#0a0a0a), gradient primary (#8b5cf6 to #3b82f6), glassmorphism cards
- Every UI frame should be screenshot-accurate to the real dashboard — builds trust
- The "cursor typing" animation on the AI email writer is the money shot — spend extra time on this
- Add subtle particle/glow effects behind the logo reveal
- Captions: auto-caption everything for silent viewing (80% of viewers watch muted)
- Export three cuts: 60-sec (YouTube/landing), 30-sec (paid social), 15-sec (Reels bumper)
